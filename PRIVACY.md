# TRADE ACADEMY Privacy Notice

TRADE ACADEMY is an educational trading simulator by **JABORZODA and MUZAFARZODA**.

## Data
The current mobile build does not require an account, broker connection, advertising SDK, analytics SDK, or payment account. Trading activity, quiz progress, journal entries and the demo plan are kept only in the current app session.

## AI
The current AI mentor is an offline, topic-aware educational assistant. Messages are not sent to an external AI service by this build.

## Financial safety
The application is educational software. It does not execute real trades and does not provide a guarantee of financial results.

For store publication, this notice should be published at a stable public URL and reviewed against the final production data practices before release.
